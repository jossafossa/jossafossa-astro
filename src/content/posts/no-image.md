Some posts don't need a hero image. This one is here to verify that the card layout, the detail page, and the grid all handle a missing image gracefully — no broken `<img>` tag, no awkward empty space, no layout shift.

It's also a reminder that not every idea needs a stock photo. A short paragraph and a clear title carry their own weight.

## A small section

When the image slot is empty, the title should sit comfortably at the top of the card, and the meta line (date, author) should follow without any visible gap where the image used to be.
